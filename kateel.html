Kateel Durga Parameshwari Temple

📍 Location

Kateel Temple is located near Mangalore in Karnataka, about 25–30 km from the city. It is uniquely situated on a small island in the Nandini River, connected by bridges on both sides.

🙏 Deity

The main deity is Goddess Durga Parameshwari, a powerful form of Adi Shakti.

She is worshipped as a protector and wish-fulfilling goddess
Devotees believe she removes obstacles and negativity
The temple is especially famous for “Chandika Yagna” and “Annadana” (free food service)

📖 History

The temple has ancient origins rooted in mythology
According to legend, a demon named Arunasura was destroying the world
Goddess Durga took the form of Parameshwari and defeated him here
The name “Kateel” comes from:
“Kati” (center) “Ila” (land) 👉 meaning “center of the land”

🛕 Daily Rituals

🌅 Morning

Suprabhata Seva, Abhisheka, Alankara, and Morning Aarti are performed to begin the day with divine energy.

🌞 Afternoon

Maha Pooja and Naivedya are offered, followed by Annadana (free food service to devotees).

🌇 Evening

Evening Aarti, devotional songs, and Ratri Pooja are performed before closing the temple.

🌙 Special Rituals

Chandika Yagna and special sevas are conducted for devotees seeking blessings and spiritual fulfillment.

🎉 Festivals

Navaratri / Dasara – Grand celebrations with decorations
Yakshagana performances (traditional dance drama) happen overnight
Special poojas attract thousands of devotees

🌟 Special Features

The temple is beautifully located on a small island in the Nandini River, creating a peaceful and scenic environment.
Annadana (free meals) is served daily to devotees, reflecting the temple’s tradition of service and devotion.
The temple is well known for cultural performances like Yakshagana during festivals.
Temple Elephant – Mahalakshmi 🐘:
Mahalakshmi is the beloved temple elephant, known for her friendly nature and interaction with devotees. She gives blessings by placing her trunk on visitors' heads, symbolizing prosperity and protection.
Mahalakshmi is also famous for her unique football-playing skills ⚽, which have attracted many visitors and made her a special highlight of the temple.
