Kateel Durga Parameshwari Temple

📍 Location

Kateel is located near Mangalore in Karnataka, on the banks of the Nandini River.

🙏 Deity

Goddess Durga Parameshwari is worshipped here with great devotion.

📖 History

The temple has ancient roots and is one of the most famous temples in coastal Karnataka.
